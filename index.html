🩹 First Aid Instructions
❤️
CPR / Cardiac Arrest
  • Call 108 immediately
  • Lay patient flat on back
  • Push hard & fast (100–120/min) on chest center
  • 2 rescue breaths every 30 compressions
  • Use AED if available
🩸
Heavy Bleeding
  • Apply firm direct pressure with clean cloth
  • Do NOT remove cloth if soaked
  • Elevate injured part above heart
  • Tourniquet for limbs if severe
🔥
Burns
  • Cool under running water 10–20 min
  • No ice, butter, or toothpaste
  • Remove jewelry near burn
  • Cover loosely with clean bandage
🫁
Choking
  • Encourage forceful coughing
  • 5 firm back blows
  • 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich)
  • Call 108 if not resolved
🧠
Stroke — FAST
  • Face drooping?
  • Arm weakness?
  • Speech slurred?
  • Time — call 108 IMMEDIATELY
🦴
Fracture
  • Do NOT straighten bone
  • Immobilize with splint
  • Apply ice wrapped in cloth
  • Get to hospital urgently
